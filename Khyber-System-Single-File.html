Service · 02

Business workflow automation for small businesses.

Make repetitive business processes easier to manage through thoughtful workflow automation.

Where to begin

Many businesses repeat the same steps when handling information, following up on tasks, or moving work between people. We begin by understanding the current process rather than assuming automation is always the answer.

What is worth automating

  • Steps that follow the same rules every time.
  • Information that is copied by hand from one place to another.
  • Reminders and follow ups that are easy to forget.
  • Routine updates that move work from one person to the next.

Decisions that need human judgment usually stay with people. We will say where automation fits and where it does not.

Our approach

  1. Map the current workflow and pain points.
  2. Identify repetitive or error prone steps.
  3. Discuss a practical scope and expected behavior.
  4. Build and review the agreed solution.

Automation should fit the business process. We will discuss limitations and compromises honestly before work begins.

Scope, pricing and terms
